<h1 align="center">cozyaar</h1>

<p align="center"><sub>student &middot; builder &middot; sheathed, but not idle</sub></p>

---

I turn messy data into things people can read: insurance fine print, campus records, market charts. Mostly Next.js, TypeScript and PostgreSQL, and I spend more time than is reasonable on the parts nobody sees: validation, read-only roles, sensible defaults.

I'd rather ship something small and sharp than something big and vague.

---

## Work

### 01 &nbsp; DataWise &nbsp;`sealed`

<!-- <img src="assets/datawise.gif" width="100%" alt="DataWise"> -->

Ask a campus database questions in plain English. Gemini drafts the SQL, a read-only role runs it, and charts come back. A what-if lab models things like attendance changes without touching a single record.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `PostgreSQL` `Gemini` &nbsp;·&nbsp; [source](https://github.com/cozyaar/datawise)

### 03 &nbsp; PolicyLens &nbsp;`released`

<!-- <img src="assets/policylens.gif" width="100%" alt="PolicyLens"> -->

Health insurance without the sales pitch. It scores policies on a radar chart, flags the clauses insurers tuck away (room-rent caps, mandatory copays), and has a chat that suggests a fit for your situation.

`Next.js` `Supabase` `Drizzle` `Gemini` &nbsp;·&nbsp; [live](https://policylens-rouge.vercel.app) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [source](https://github.com/cozyaar/policylens)
